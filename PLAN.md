# Portrait Alternatives

## Chosen direction

- Chosen branch: C, with a warm cheek gradient and Bézier-curved facial expression.
- C best met its criterion of communicating warmth despite unusual colors. Compared with A's bead grid and B's moon-and-flower arrangement, the gradient and curved features made the face feel more expressive while staying clear as a portrait.
- A and B remain available on their respective branches.

## A

- Explored on branch A. The build used a regular bead grid rather than the planned angular tiles, hair, clothing, and spiky halo; it still created a lively repeated pattern, but it did not read as a full portrait.
- I intend to make a portrait from 100+ visible shapes: small beads and angular tiles will form the face, hair, eyes, and clothing, with a spiky halo arranged around the head.
- The dense repeated shapes should give the portrait a lively, energetic, handmade impression.
- Criterion: reads clearly at small size.

## B

- Explored on branch B. The build used a white crescent moon and a flower, rather than the initially imagined contrasting moon and flower; the images visibly framed the face and added the intended personal detail.
- I intend to place two distinct images in the portrait: a moon in the upper background behind the head and a small flower near the lower shoulder.
- I expect to draw both images myself as simple contrasting illustrations, using the moon to frame the face and the flower to add a personal detail.
- Criterion: suits a reflective character.

## C

- Explored on branch C. The build put the peach-to-rose gradient on one cheek and used Bézier curves for the smile and nose, rather than shading the whole head from golden yellow through coral to deep blue.
- I intend to use a warm-to-cool gradient across the head, moving from golden yellow on one side through coral to deep blue on the other.
- A Bézier curve will depict a broad curved smile, while the gradient will shade the face and hair to make the expression feel dimensional.
- Criterion: communicates warmth despite the unusual colors.
